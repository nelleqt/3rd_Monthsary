# Install this redesigned version

1. Extract the new ZIP into a NEW folder. Open monthsary-webpage in VS Code.
2. If you already personalized the old project, copy your old config.js into this new folder to replace the sample config.js. Keep your password numeric (for example "0703") and update its hint to match.
3. Copy your personal first-chat screenshot and couple photos from the OLD assets folder into this new assets folder. Keep the four included design images as well.
4. Save and open the NEW index.html in your browser. Refresh with Ctrl+Shift+R if needed.

The redesign adds the uploaded artwork, comic panels, floating hero artwork, gentle panel entrances, a pulsing final heart, and reduced-motion support. The password keypad has numbers 0–9, Clear, and Delete, plus Show/Hide. Click Unlock after entering the date.

The Bible screen is now Ecclesiastes 4:12 with a cross and a short reflection, followed by a new closing message. START-HERE.md still explains the original configuration and photo setup; this file supersedes its older description of typing the password and showing the complete Bible passage.

Your personal changes made on your computer were not available here, so this download includes the original sample answers and letter. Copying your old config.js and personal photos preserves your work.
